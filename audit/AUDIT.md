## What's working
- Strong, polished project imagery. The mockups are worth reusing.
- The Roqqu case study shows real outcomes (45% faster onboarding, 101,045 Qash transactions).
- Clear, simple navigation.

## What's holding it back
1. **The positioning undersells you.** "Digital Designer" sits below the CV's "Senior Product Designer, 6+ yrs, fintech / crypto / platforms". The headline should say what you are and where you win.
2. **3 of 5 projects are locked.** Visitors hit dead ends, and the locked ones (Glover, Clink, Edubanc) hold some of your best numbers.
3. **The case studies read like process diaries.** They're long sections of "Understanding the user → Framing → …" with the outcome buried at the end. EN1 has no metrics at all. Hiring managers skim, so results should come first.
4. **Your strongest numbers aren't on the site:** +30% revenue (Glover Business), −30% drop-off, +25% retention (STEMuli), ₦45M+ (Edubanc), +18% trade completion and +22% activation (Roqqu), +15% design-system velocity (EN1).
5. **The tool stack section is filler.** Slack, Asana and Zapier don't help a senior designer stand out.
6. **The CV link goes to a Google Doc.** A PDF hosted on the site is more professional.
7. **Housekeeping:** the trial font licence, "Conlusion" typo, "it's" → "its", inconsistent dates, generic meta description ("Welcome to my online design portfolio"), no social preview image, Framer branding.
