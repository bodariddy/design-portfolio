# HUMAN.md (human-md.ai)
**Tags:** `hero` `colour` `typography` `motion` `copy` · **Status:** 🟢 steal (spirit, not literal)
**Why you picked it:** creative and unexpected, "go wild and break stereotypes".

## What it does
- **Art-directed staged scenes:** real props (fern in a pink foot planter, orange lamp, striped pyjamas) against flat, saturated colour walls. It feels like a fashion editorial, not a tech site.
- **Colour fields as chapters:** cobalt blue → bubblegum pink → forest green. Each scroll chapter changes the whole world.
- **Giant words revealed with scroll:** "humans", "machines". Short phrases build up line by line ("now that the… machines… are very good").
- **Type trio:** Bricolage Grotesque (bold display) + Instrument Serif (the ".md") + Cutive Mono (tiny typewriter notes).
- **Load sequence:** dark → blue field → a single red dot → scene and wordmark appear.
- **Small details:** red dot accents, a coffee mug icon as the logo, a mono "last edited at the kitchen table, coffee #2" caption.
- Very long scroll-driven page (≈480k px). It's a manifesto, not a portfolio.

## Take
- Bold flat colour fields per chapter or case study. Your old covers already have colour worlds: Roqqu orange, Clink blue, Glover purple, EN1 black.
- A display grotesk + elegant serif + mono trio.
- Mono "margin notes" that carry personality.
- Scroll-built sentences for the intro statement.

## Avoid
- The length. Hiring managers won't scroll 480k px. Keep the theatre to the hero and transitions, and make the case studies scannable.
- Copying the photographic props style literally. We'd need our own art direction.
