**Why you picked it:** positions him as a design engineer who goes from design to code with tools. Great interactions.

## What it does
- **Positioning:** "Product designer & design engineer". "Designed, then built. One person on both sides of the handoff, so the details survive."
- **Hero = a live design tool.** It shows a pixel ruler / 8-col grid overlay, a magnifier loupe over a button showing its CSS, and sliders (radius, size, weight) that restyle the button live. "Runs itself. Drag to take over."
- **"The build" scroll story, pinned:** a percentage counter (000% → 099%) while a mockup goes Design → Build (code panel) → Engineering (pull request) → Review → Live. A toolbar of steps sits underneath. Honest footnote: "I use AI tools to build faster. The decisions stay mine."
- **Principles with code snippets** (`contrast: 5.9:1 /* AA */`, `handoffs: 0`): each claim is proven in code.
- Isometric layered "Foundations / Product / Craft" diagram for skills.
- Peer testimonials ("Kind words").
- Space Grotesk + Bricolage + JetBrains Mono on #F4F4F2, with a single terracotta accent #C6613F. Lots of tiny mono labels and coordinates (`x 270 · y 120`).

## Take
- **Show the build side, not just claim it.** Interactive hero details, tokens and code next to design.
- **"Runs itself, drag to take over":** autoplay that hands control to the visitor. Our terminal already does this, so we can lean in.
- **Testimonials section.** We don't have one. `[TOSIN: can you get 2–3 quotes from PMs or engineers you've worked with?]`
- The honest AI line.

## ⚠️ Differentiate
- **He's a Nigerian fintech designer who also worked at Roqqu,** with a Roqqu case study ("KYC 40% → 65%"). Hiring managers in the same market may see both. Your Roqqu story must clearly state *your* scope, and our site must not look like his.
- **His tagline, "making products people trust", is close to our current headline.** Change ours.
- Don't copy "I've got your back with…", the process-step pinned story, or the ruler/grid look. Both of these reference sites already share that phrasing.
