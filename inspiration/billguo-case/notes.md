# Bill Guo case study: New Craft Society (billguo.me/work/ncs)
**Tags:** `case-study` `layout` `colour` · **Status:** 🟢 adopted

## Structure
- Split hero: centred title block on the left (outlined category tag, serif title, one-line outcome, "NCS · 5 MIN READ"); product image bleeding off the right edge on a soft painterly plate.
- Meta + intro: labels stacked on the left (Industry, Timeline, Status, Team), intro paragraph on the right.
- Narrow centred reading column (~560–640px). Small mono labels with a coloured dot ("PROBLEM", "UPLOAD") above each heading.
- Big chapters (Solutions, Research, Process…) open with a dashed hairline divider + centred mono label, and a centred serif question.
- Media wider than the text, on soft green bokeh plates.
- Sticky bottom navigator: back · current chapter + progress dots · prev/next.

## Palette (light)
Paper #fcf9f4-ish, ink ~#2a2a2a at 90%, muted warm grey ~#74695e, hairlines ~20% grey, 3–4% ink tint for surfaces.

## Adopted ✅
All of the above, with our coin gold replacing his green on every plate. Light palette now site-wide.
