# David Ukauwa CV (commissioner.design)
**Tags:** `cv` `typography` · **Status:** 🟢 steal (tone and layout details)

## What it does
- Quiet, editorial typography: a light serif (BIZ UDPMincho) in soft greys, with lots of air. It feels like a designed object, not a template.
- **Title line in letter-spaced caps:** "SENIOR DESIGN ENGINEER · PRODUCT DESIGNER · CO-FOUNDER".
- **Contact on a single tiny caps line,** separated by slashes.
- **Role row:** **Company** + role in grey + linked company domain, with dates right-aligned in small caps.
- Skills as **outlined chips** grouped into Design / Research / Tools / Technical.
- Hand-drawn **wavy hairline dividers** between sections.
- Extra sections: **Awards & recognition**, **Community & publications**.
- **Handwritten signature** at the bottom left, city at the bottom right.

## Take for Tosin's CV
Spaced-caps title line, one-line contact, company + grey role, skill chips, soft wavy dividers, a Community section (mentoring), and a signature sign-off.

## Avoid
- Grey-on-white body text that's too light for print and some ATS-to-PDF viewers. Keep the body near-black.
- Including a phone number (your call: no phone).
