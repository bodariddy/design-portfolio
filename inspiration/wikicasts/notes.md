# Wikicasts (usewikicasts.com)
**Tags:** `colour` `hero` `detail` `copy` · **Status:** 🟢 steal (bottom gradient, done)
**Why you picked it:** overall feel, and the gradient at the bottom.

## What it does
- **Rotating hero word:** "Make a cast about [🌋 volcanoes]". The topic swaps every couple of seconds inside a soft pill with a tiny image.
- **Warm off-white (#F1EEE9) ↔ near-black,** switching theme per section as you scroll. A phone mockup rises from the bottom of the hero.
- **Read-along:** "every word lights up as it's said", the same idea as our scrolling statement.
- **Features as a quiet 4-column grid** of line icons and two-line descriptions. Calm, very Apple.
- **Footer aurora:** layered `linear-gradient(to top, violet → transparent)` glows rising from the bottom edge behind "What are you curious about?". Dark version is deep violet, light version is a lavender haze.
- System font, tiny type, glass pills (`linear-gradient(155deg, white .66 → .26)` with blur).

## Taken ✅
- **Aurora behind the contact section** on every page, built from our project colours (Roqqu indigo → Qash violet → Glover hibiscus → coin gold → Clink lagoon), slowly drifting, softer in light mode, stronger in dark.
- **Glass buttons** over the aurora.
