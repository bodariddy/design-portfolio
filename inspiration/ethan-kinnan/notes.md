- Muted slate/sage text colours that sit inside the painting's palette.
- Warm cream (#FBF8F2) shows before the image loads.

## Take

- A live, ticking detail (his age). For you it could be "years designing" or a live Lagos clock.
- A hidden playful interaction as a reward for curious visitors.
