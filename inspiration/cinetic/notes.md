## What it does
- **A full-screen showreel intro:** fast cuts of cinematic footage (rally car, skier, F1, watches, drama). A small **"Skip intro"** pill sits at the bottom centre. No UI until it ends.
- Black background, **Zalando Sans Expanded** (wide, uppercase) for a big manifesto: "WE ARE A CREATIVE HOUSE SHAPING ADVERTISING, CINEMA AND EXPERIENCE DESIGN, THAT MOVE PEOPLE."
- Work split into three big categories (Advertising, Cinema, Experience Design), each "Discover all the projects". A "JOIN THE MOVEMENT" contact.
- 22 videos on the homepage. Heavy, scroll-driven, made for an agency selling film.

## Taken ✅
- **Visible "Skip intro ↵" pill on our loader** (click, Enter or Esc). Our intro was already short and skippable, but now it says so.
