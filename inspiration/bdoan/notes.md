# Ben Doan (bdoan.me)
**Tags:** `layout` `motion` `case-study` · **Status:** 🟢 steal (project grid) · 🟡 maybe (scroll scenes)
**Why you picked it:** the project layout.

## What it does
- **Projects are a full-bleed, edge-to-edge 3×2 grid:** no gutters, no rounded cards. Each tile is a dark image with the title + ↗ and the tech stack in tiny text at the bottom-left.
- **Hover spotlight:** the hovered tile brightens to full colour and the others stay dimmed. It's cinematic and very easy to scan.
- Scroll-driven scenes before the grid: the name over a landscape, a rainbow ribbon, a day→night transition, a "torn paper" grid of interest photos, and a giant 3D "PROJECTS" word set into a city skyline.
- Navy-black (#0E172E) base, Anton display + Inter Tight body.

## Take
- **The full-bleed tile grid + hover spotlight** for your selected work: 4–5 tiles, each showing the company, outcome and one metric.
- Giant section-title moments ("WORK") as a transition.

## Avoid
- The long chain of scroll scenes before the work. The work should be one scroll away.
- Tiny low-contrast metadata. Our tile text has to pass contrast in both themes.
