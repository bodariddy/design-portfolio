## What it does
- **Hero:** "I design products you feel something about, / Then I build them." A two-line promise: design, then build.
- **Fixed left sidebar:** nav + every social link + "12:26 local time · Manchester, UK" (you'll recognise our Lagos clock).
- **Collage hero:** a cut-out portrait with stickers of his interests (games controller, camera, car, cartoon). Personality before work.
- **Work as a long scrolling list:** a coloured logo, one line, roles + years, then big project imagery with "paper clip / tape" scrapbook details.
- **About written as a letter:** "Hello, I'm David. Thanks for stopping by." … "Cheers," + handwritten signature.
- **GitHub contribution graph** ("768 contributions in the last year"): proof that he builds.
- "Where I've been" timeline, awards list.
- **"Off the clock":** Cats, Painting, Running (linked to Strava), "Last played on Spotify", and a **playable Pong game** at the bottom.
- BIZ UDPMincho (Japanese serif) everywhere at small sizes. Quiet, editorial, near-white #F7F7F7.

## Take
- **About as a personal letter with a signature.** Your old About story fits this perfectly.
- **Off the clock:** working out, video games, friends, mentoring. Make it alive: "currently playing" a game, "currently lifting" a stat, mentoring sessions.
- **A small playable easter egg.** On the terminal, a hidden "game" channel would be on-brand for you as a gamer.
- **Proof of building:** a GitHub graph or "Built by me" colophon if we go the design-engineer route.

## Avoid
- The sidebar link list (10 links) is noisy. Keep socials to 3–4.
- The tiny serif body text hurts readability.
