Project Overview
In the growing electric vehicle (EV) market, EN1 aspired to be the leading name in Nigeria. To achieve this, they needed more than just a cutting-edge product—they required a compelling digital presence that could connect with potential customers, elevate their brand, and drive conversions. My role as Product designer was to execute their digital transformation, ensuring that the digital experience mirrored the luxury and performance of their vehicles.

Website & Mobile App:
Designed a minimalist homepage with high-quality images and smooth interactions, making it easy for users to explore vehicles while feeling the premium nature of the brand.
Developed a car configurator, enabling users to visualize their ideal vehicle—color, trim, features—in real time. This added a personal touch, letting customers feel connected to their purchase.
Created a kitshop for EN1-branded merchandise, reinforcing brand identity and allowing for additional revenue streams.

Admin Portal:
Built a simple, intuitive admin dashboard for EN1’s internal team to manage car orders, merchandise, and customer inquiries.
The configurator let’s users personalise their car by selecting preferences such as paint, wheel type and lighting.

Finding structure:
Elevating a Premium Electric Vehicle Brand in Nigeria
How do you create a luxury digital experience that feels premium and resonates locally
While the project is still ongoing, the design direction positioned EN1 as not just an automotive brand, but as a statement of lifestyle and innovation. For me, this project reinforced how important it is to design experiences that balance aspiration with context — especially when introducing premium products into emerging markets.
User insights:
Audience: Affluent, tech-forward, sustainability-conscious Nigerians
Motivations: Prestige, innovation, environmental responsibility
Barriers: High cost, infrastructure concerns (charging, support)
Approach
Created user-flows: awareness → exploration → purchase
Designed experience architecture for digital touch-points
Designed the EN1 design system
Balanced local context with global luxury standards
Role & Responsibility
I was responsible for:
Visual Design: Creating a cohesive, luxury-focused aesthetic for the entire digital experience.
User Flows & Prototyping: Mapping out user journeys across the website, mobile app, configurator, and internal portals.
Collaboration: Working closely with developers and stakeholders to ensure the design translated well into the final product.
EN1 was targeting Nigerian consumers—a market driven by rising fuel prices and growing interest in sustainable transportation options. My task was to craft a user-centric digital platform that would:
Showcase EN1’s electric vehicles in a compelling, premium way.
Allow users to explore and customize vehicles.
Promote brand merchandise and generate additional revenue.
Create a backend system to streamline operations for EN1’s internal team.
This experience taught me how to adapt global design standards to a local market context — something I see as a recurring theme in my work
