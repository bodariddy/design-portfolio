How dividends work on Roqqu
When a company you're invested in pays a dividend, you get your share of it — automatically. No forms, no claims, nothing to set up.
What you receive
We credit your USD wallet with the full value of your dividend, based on the shares you held on the ex-dividend date. The amount follows the same math the company uses: rate per share × your shares, including fractions.
What happens behind the scenes
Roqqu handles the processing on your behalf — collecting the payment, applying any required withholding tax, and converting it into a credit to your wallet. That's why your dividend arrives as a single, ready-to-use amount.
When it arrives
Dividends are credited on or shortly after the company's official payment date. You'll see each one move from Announced to Paid right on this tab, and we'll notify you when it lands.
Taxes
Some dividends are subject to withholding tax before they reach you. When that happens, we show you the gross amount, the tax deducted, and what you received — no silent deductions.
Dividend amounts are estimates until paid and may change based on the company's final declaration, applicable taxes, or exchange rates. Roqqu credits the equivalent value of the dividend to your wallet.
