Stake crypto to earn up to 2.34 APY
Staking earns yield on assets you already hold. Roqqu never takes custody — your keys sign every action on this device.
How it works
Pick an amount and confirm with Face ID. Your assets are delegated to a validator.
Activation
Your stake goes live at the start of the next epoch — usually within ~2 days.
Earn
Rewards accrue automatically each epoch. Watch them grow under My positions.
Unstake
Unstake anytime. A network unbonding wait applies (~3 days for SOL) — no rewards during it.
Withdraw
Once unbonding ends, withdraw your assets back to your wallet.
What you should know
Direct deposit
top up your account from your banking application
APY is variable
Set by the network, not Roqqu.
Unbonding periods differ
~3 days SOL · ~9–14 days ETH.
Minimums & fees apply
Small gas fee; SOL keeps a ~0.002 rent reserve.
Rewards aren't guaranteed
Depends on network and validator uptime.
