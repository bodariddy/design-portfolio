## How I work
_Short and scannable. Shows seniority without buzzwords._

**Start with the problem, not the screen.**
I run discovery before design: research synthesis, journey mapping and problem-framing workshops. Teams agree on *why* before debating *what*.

**Design for high-stakes moments.**
Money movement, identity checks, irreversible actions. I design these flows to be clear when users are nervous, which is where trust is won or lost.

**Build systems, not just screens.**
From EN1's first design system to Roqqu's trading surfaces, I build foundations that let teams ship faster and stay consistent.

**Measure, then iterate.**
I partner with PM and data to define success up front, then validate with usability tests before launch and metrics after.
