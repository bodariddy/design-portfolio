## Where it started
I grew up in my father's study, playing computer games and learning to type on his machine. I was hooked less by the games than by how some products left a lasting impression and others didn't. That curiosity led me to a Computer Science degree at the Federal University of Agriculture, Abeokuta, and from there into design.

The engineering background still shapes how I work. I think in systems, I speak developers' language, and I care about what's feasible as much as what's beautiful.

## What I believe
- **Clarity is a feature.** Especially when people's money is on the line.
- **Good design is measurable.** If it doesn't move a metric or remove real pain, it's decoration.
- **Systems scale people.** A good design system gives a team its time back.
- **Ownership over output.** I'd rather own a problem than polish a ticket.

## What I bring to a team
- End-to-end product design, from discovery and strategy to launch

- Founder experience: I co-founded Clink and owned the product, growth and experiments
