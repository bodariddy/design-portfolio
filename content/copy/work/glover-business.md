## TL;DR
- **Problem:** Glover's consumer gift card app had high drop-off, and businesses wanting to buy gift cards in bulk (for rewards, incentives, gifting) had no dedicated product. `[CONFIRM]`
- **What I did:** Led design of Glover Business from scratch and redesigned Glover Lite across web and mobile. I also worked with engineering on transaction security UX and scalability.
- **Results:**
  - **+30%** revenue (Glover Business)
  - **−30%** user drop-off (Glover Lite)
  - Improved brand consistency and engagement across the platform
