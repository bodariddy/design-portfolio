# Content strategy (draft v1)

## Audience
Hiring managers, design leads and recruiters hiring **senior / lead product designers** for full-time roles, mostly at fintech, crypto and platform companies. They skim on desktop between meetings, look at 1–2 case studies at most, and are judging three things:
1. Can this person own a problem end to end?
2. Do they move business metrics?
3. Can they work with PM and engineering, and lead?

**Primary action:** email / book a call. **Secondary:** download the CV (PDF hosted on the site).

## Positioning
> Senior Product Designer, 6+ years in fintech and crypto. I make high-risk financial products feel simple, and I measure the result.

Proof points, in order: crypto trading at scale (Roqqu) · 0→1 platform plus a design system (EN1) · founder (Clink) · revenue (Glover Business) · Weave _(TBC)_.

## Metric strip (homepage, real numbers only)
`+30% revenue` (Glover Business) · `+22% activation` (Roqqu) · `−35% onboarding time` (Clink) · `+15% team velocity` (EN1 DS) · `₦45M+ processed` (Edubanc)

## Case-study template (results first, readable in 90 seconds)
1. **Header:** title as an outcome ("Cutting onboarding time 45% at Roqqu"), role, team, timeline, platform
2. **TL;DR card:** problem · what I did · results (3 metrics)
3. **Context:** the business problem and constraints (2–3 short paragraphs)
4. **My role:** what I owned and who I worked with
5. **Key decisions (2–4):** each one is the insight, then the decision, then visuals, then the trade-off
6. **Results:** metrics, plus one qualitative signal (a quote, adoption)
7. **Reflection:** what I'd do differently (shows seniority)
8. **Next case study →**
